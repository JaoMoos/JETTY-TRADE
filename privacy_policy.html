Privacy Policy

App: RecallPath
Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RecallPath ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application. RecallPath is an offline flashcard and spaced repetition study app.

Summary: RecallPath does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data to us or to third-party analytics or advertising services. Your study data stays on your device unless you choose to export it yourself.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information on our servers. RecallPath has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend database operated by us.

The app stores the following data locally on your device only to provide its features:

  • Flashcard decks and cards you create or import
  • Spaced repetition study progress (scheduling state, review history, daily activity summaries)
  • App preferences (such as theme and optional reminder settings)
  • Onboarding completion status

This locally stored information is not sent to us and is not shared with third parties by the app.

2. Permissions Used by the App

RecallPath requests only the permissions needed for optional features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13 and above): Requested only if you turn on daily study reminders in Settings. If you deny this permission or leave reminders off, the app works normally without sending notifications.

RecallPath does not declare the following permissions:

  • Internet access (INTERNET)
  • Camera or microphone (including audio recording)
  • Location
  • Contacts, SMS, phone, or calendar
  • Storage read/write permissions for broad file access

Import and export use the Android system file picker (Storage Access Framework). You choose the file location; the app accesses only the file you select for that action.

Some Android system libraries included with the app (for example, local background task scheduling) may add standard device permissions to the installed application package, such as wake lock or network state access. RecallPath does not use these permissions to collect or transmit your personal data, and the app does not perform network requests for its core functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Locally stored data is used only to:

  • Display and manage your decks and cards
  • Schedule and record review sessions
  • Show statistics such as streaks and activity summaries
  • Apply your in-app settings
  • Optionally show local study reminders

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. RecallPath does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs that collect personal information.

If you export a backup file, you control where that file is saved or shared. Any sharing after export is outside the app and under your control.

5. Text-to-Speech (Listening Study Mode)

Listening mode uses your device's built-in text-to-speech (TTS) engine to read card text aloud. RecallPath does not record your voice and does not send audio to us. TTS processing is handled by your device or the TTS provider configured in your system settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Data & Backup → Clear all data.

Android automatic backup for RecallPath is disabled (allowBackup=false). To move data to another device, use Export all data and Import backup in Settings.

7. Security

Data is stored in the app's private local storage on your device. Protection at rest depends on your device's operating system security (such as device encryption and screen lock). RecallPath does not transmit your study data over the internet.

8. Children's Privacy

RecallPath is a general study tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information on our servers, no child-specific data is transmitted to us.

9. Your Choices

  • Use the app without enabling notifications
  • Export your data as a local backup file
  • Clear all app data from Settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device

10. International Users

Because RecallPath operates offline and does not transfer your study data to our servers, your information generally remains on your device in your country of use.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Google Play Data Safety Summary

For Google Play Data Safety disclosures, RecallPath aligns with the following:

  • Data collected: None by the developer
  • Data shared: None
  • Data encrypted in transit: Not applicable (no developer server transmission)
  • Data deletion: Available in-app via Clear all data
  • Android backup: Disabled
